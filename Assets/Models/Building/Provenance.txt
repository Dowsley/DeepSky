Habitat module meshes

Deck, WallPanel, WindowFrame, Ceiling, Ladder and Support are original geometry
created for DeepSky. Their editable Blender source is retained locally in
Art/Source/Habitat.blend, with the authoring script beside it.

The materials reuse Assets/Textures/Equipment/FieldEquipmentAtlas.png, an original
AI-generated texture made without supplied reference images. It is not a CC0
download. This provenance does not establish exclusivity or copyright eligibility.
The atlas's original generation record is retained locally in Art/FieldEquipment.md.

Window-pane and entrance-water geometry, and their shaders, are original project
work. Reference-game geometry and textures are not included in this kit.
